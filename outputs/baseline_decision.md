**Pros**:

**Cons**:

**Description**: Originate standard 24-to-36 month SME term loans to meet genuine market demand across retail, service, and manufacturing sectors. To comply with the 1-year pilot boundary, institutional loan assignment agreements are executed with partner banks at Month 10 to offload remaining principal balance while retaining fee spread.

**Pros**:
- Attracts higher-quality borrowers by providing tenure that matches true capital investment needs
- Monetizes origination fees and ongoing servicing spreads without holding multi-year balance sheet risk
**Cons**:
- High counterparty reliance on secondary institutional buyers to execute portfolio buyout at Month 10
- Increased regulatory compliance overhead regarding loan assignment guidelines
